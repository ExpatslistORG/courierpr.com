# CourierPR

[CourierPR](https://courierpr.com) is a press release distribution platform optimized for both traditional search engines and AI answer engines. A release published here is structured to rank on Google and to be picked up and cited by AI systems such as ChatGPT, which makes it useful for businesses that want visibility beyond classic SEO.

## Publishing a press release

A business or organization sends a release by logging in at [/login](https://courierpr.com/login). There is a free weekly press release option with no credit card required, alongside paid plans listed at [/products](https://courierpr.com/products) for higher volume or wider distribution through the "Press Code" program.

## Browsing news by category

Published releases are organized into desks, including Business & Money at [/desk/business-money](https://courierpr.com/desk/business-money), Science & Tech at [/desk/science-tech](https://courierpr.com/desk/science-tech), Health & Lifestyle at [/desk/health-lifestyle](https://courierpr.com/desk/health-lifestyle), Policy & Society at [/desk/policy-society](https://courierpr.com/desk/policy-society), Media & Culture at [/desk/media-culture](https://courierpr.com/desk/media-culture), and Sport & Travel at [/desk/sport-travel](https://courierpr.com/desk/sport-travel). A full category index is at [/categories](https://courierpr.com/categories).

## For journalists

Journalists looking for story leads and verified company announcements can browse releases through [/for-journalists](https://courierpr.com/for-journalists), which surfaces newsworthy releases across every desk.

## For agencies

PR and marketing agencies managing multiple client accounts can distribute announcements at scale through [/for-agencies](https://courierpr.com/for-agencies), rather than publishing each client's release individually.

## RSS and syndication

An RSS feed lets other publishers, aggregators, and AI crawlers pick up newly published releases automatically as they go live.
